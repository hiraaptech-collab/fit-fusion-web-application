Understand Your Results

BMI Categories

BMI is a helpful starting point for understanding your body composition. Here's what each range means.

Below 18.5
Underweight

You may need more calories and nutrient-dense foods to reach a healthy weight.

18.5 – 24.9
Normal Weight

You're in a healthy range — maintain balance with good nutrition and exercise.

25 – 29.9
Overweight

A balanced diet and regular activity can help you move toward a healthier range.

30 and above
Obese

Structured guidance and professional support are recommended to improve health.

Wellness Tips

Beyond The Number

Your BMI is just one piece of the puzzle. Use these habits to support overall wellness.

Balanced Nutrition

Prioritize whole foods, lean protein, and plenty of fruits and vegetables.

Stay Active

Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate exercise every week.

Stay Hydrated

Drink 8+ glasses of water daily to support energy and recovery.

Sleep Well

Get 7–9 hours of quality sleep to support weight and mood regulation.
